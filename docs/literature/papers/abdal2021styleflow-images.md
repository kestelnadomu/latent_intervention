# StyleFlow: Attribute-conditioned Exploration of StyleGAN-Generated Images using Conditional Continuous Normalizing Flows

Rameen Abdal; Peihao Zhu; Niloy J. Mitra; Peter Wonka. 2021. ACM Transactions on Graphics 40(3), article 21; 2020 preprint.

- [Author project and publication metadata](https://rameenabdal.github.io/StyleFlow/)
- [Preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.02401)

## Project relevance (triage)

A close algorithmic neighbor to `state_flow`: attribute-conditioned continuous flows edit GAN latents through reverse inference followed by forward inference under changed attributes.

## Notes and caveats

This establishes an attribute-editing precedent, not identification of an SCM's individual counterfactual. It is a different work from [Zhu et al.'s text StyleFlow](zhu2024styleflow.md); do not merge the two because they share an acronym.
