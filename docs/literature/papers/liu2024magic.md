# Multi-Aspect Controllable Text Generation with Disentangled Counterfactual Augmentation

Yi Liu; Xiangyu Liu; Xiangrong Zhu; Wei Hu. 2024. ACL, Long Papers, pp. 9231–9253.

- [Proceedings](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.500/)

## Project relevance (triage)

Uses counterfactual feature augmentation to address correlated attributes in multi-aspect text control, with target-guided augmentation at inference. Relevant to edits that must change one semantic aspect without unintended shifts in others.

## Notes and caveats

Attribute disentanglement and controllability do not establish structural causal identification. Its text-generation setup is not an unchanged arbitrary downstream predictor operating on edited CV vectors.
