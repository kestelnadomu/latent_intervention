# Post-hoc Stochastic Concept Bottleneck Models

Wiktor Jan Hoffmann; Sonia Laguna; Moritz Vandenhirtz; Emanuele Palumbo; Julia E. Vogt. 2025 preprint; screened revision v4 dated 2026-03-14. Venue not verified.

- [Preprint and version history](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.08219)

## Project relevance (triage)

A lightweight covariance-prediction module adds dependent concept uncertainty to an existing CBM without retraining its backbone. Particularly relevant to modular semantic-decoder alternatives.

## Notes and caveats

It presupposes a pretrained concept bottleneck and does not itself provide a latent counterfactual renderer. Publication status remains unverified; retain the distinction between the 2025 first posting and 2026 revision.
