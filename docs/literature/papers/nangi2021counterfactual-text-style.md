# Counterfactuals to Control Latent Disentangled Text Representations for Style Transfer

Sharmila Reddy Nangi; Niyati Chhaya; Sopan Khosla; Nikhil Kaushik; Harshit Nyati. 2021. ACL-IJCNLP, Short Papers, pp. 40–48.

- [Proceedings](https://aclanthology.org/2021.acl-short.7/)

## Project relevance (triage)

Uses counterfactual-based modification of disentangled text representations to control style and its strength. A direct text-latent editing predecessor beyond generic activation steering.

## Notes and caveats

Learned content/style representations and style-transfer evaluation differ from a frozen general-purpose CV encoder and known structural counterfactual pairs. Avoid treating the word counterfactual as proof of SCM semantics.
