# Interpretable Generative Models through Post-hoc Concept Bottlenecks

Akshay Kulkarni; Ge Yan; Chung-En Sun; Tuomas Oikarinen; Tsui-Wei Weng. 2025. CVPR, pp. 8162–8171.

- [Proceedings](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Kulkarni_Interpretable_Generative_Models_through_Post-hoc_Concept_Bottlenecks_CVPR_2025_paper.html)
- [Author project](https://lilywenglab.github.io/posthoc-generative-cbm/)

## Project relevance (triage)

Concept-bottleneck autoencoders and concept controllers add semantic editing to frozen generative models. This is a close comparator for modular concept readout and rendering, not merely generic concept-bottleneck background.

## Notes and caveats

The setting uses generative-model representations and image synthesis; our renderer edits document embeddings with an externally defined symbolic operation. Do not claim that frozen-backbone semantic editing is unique to this project.
