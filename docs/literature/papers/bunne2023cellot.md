# Learning single-cell perturbation responses using neural optimal transport

Charlotte Bunne et al. 2023. Nature Methods 20:1759–1768.

## Project relevance (triage)

Important precedent for learning sample-level responses from unpaired factual/perturbed populations with neural OT. Its biological setting and distributional validation do not establish recovery of a particular unit's unobserved counterpart in our setting.
