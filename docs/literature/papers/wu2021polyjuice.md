# Polyjuice: Generating Counterfactuals for Explaining, Evaluating, and Improving Models

Tongshuang Wu, Marco Tulio Ribeiro, Jeffrey Heer and Daniel Weld. 2021. ACL.

- [Primary source](https://aclanthology.org/2021.acl-long.523/)

## Project relevance (triage)

Text-counterfactual generation precedent relevant when evaluating whether source-guided CV editing would be a new contribution or a different task from latent-only inversion.

## Notes and caveats

Polyjuice trains a controllable generator for textual perturbations; its counterfactual objective and supervision are not the project's frozen-latent SCM intervention setup.
