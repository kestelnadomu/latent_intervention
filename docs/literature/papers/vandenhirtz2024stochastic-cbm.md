# Stochastic Concept Bottleneck Models

Moritz Vandenhirtz; Sonia Laguna; Ričards Marcinkevičs; Julia E. Vogt. 2024. NeurIPS.

- [Proceedings](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/5c7894ac8788555f1cecf536f1e0fd35-Abstract-Conference.html)
- [Preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.19272)

## Project relevance (triage)

Models dependence and uncertainty among concepts rather than treating concept predictions independently. Relevant to the independent-versus-joint semantic-decoder comparison and the propagation of reader uncertainty.

## Notes and caveats

Updating correlated concepts after observing a correction is not the same operation as applying an SCM intervention. This is a probabilistic-reader comparator, not evidence that concept correlations identify causal effects.
