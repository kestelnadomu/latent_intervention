# The Curious Case of Neural Text Degeneration

Ari Holtzman, Jan Buys, Li Du, Maxwell Forbes and Yejin Choi. 2020. ICLR; 2019 preprint.

- [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.09751)

## Project relevance (triage)

Generation-strategy background for distinguishing decoder quality from the choice of greedy or stochastic decoding in long CV outputs.

## Notes and caveats

The paper motivates nucleus sampling for open-ended generation. More fluent or diverse CVs would not automatically be more faithful to a particular latent vector.
